Privacy policy

Pinecone Songs Uploader · Effective 3 October 2026

Who we are

Pinecone Songs Uploader is a private tool run by the owner of the Pinecone Songs YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner, to manage that channel. Contact: zarbot.ea.mt5@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for access to the owner's own YouTube account through these permissions:

  • YouTube upload and manage (youtube, youtube.upload): to upload our videos, and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to list our own videos and playlists.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): to read our own channel's views, watch time and revenue reports.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to publish and improve the Pinecone Songs channel.
  • The sign-in token and analytics reports are stored on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers of the channel, about children, or about any other Google user. It never signs in anyone except the channel owner.
  • The app does not use the data for advertising, and does not transfer it to third parties except as needed to call Google's own APIs.

Google API Services

Pinecone Songs Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes the stored access as well.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
